Click the arena first so it takes your keys. WS move, AD turn, QE strafe, Space jumps. Left-drag looks around, right-drag steers (A/D then strafe), both buttons run forward, the wheel zooms. Click the dummy or press Tab to target it, Esc clears. Drag spells from the Spellbook onto any bar; drag a button off a bar to remove it. Right-click a pet ability to toggle autocast. Browsers swallow a few combos (Ctrl+1–9, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T): use the modifier latches in Key Bind Mode to assign those, they are saved even if they can't fire here.
